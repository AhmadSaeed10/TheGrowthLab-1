A rebuild is the most expensive answer to a slow store. Before you commission one, check these five things.

## 1. Find where visitors leave

Look at the path from first page to checkout and note where most people drop. If they leave at the product page, redesigning the homepage will not help.

## 2. Fix the product page first

The product page does most of the selling. Check that the first screen shows the product, the price and the buy button on a phone, and that the questions buyers ask most are answered without scrolling far.

## 3. Review it on a phone

Open the store on a real phone on mobile data. If a menu, a pop-up or an app widget gets in the way, that is your first fix.

## 4. Remove what you do not use

Old apps add weight and clutter. List every installed app and remove the ones nobody can explain.

## 5. Confirm your tracking

Before you spend on ads, check that purchases are recorded correctly in your ad accounts. Campaigns can only optimize against what they can see.
