Paul Carter sells a mood as much as a garment: resort light, clean tailoring, a little theatre. The job was to keep that mood identical everywhere a customer meets the brand.

On social, campaign imagery like Winter Vol.1 is designed as a set, so the grid reads like a magazine. On the storefront, the same photography leads the homepage, with navigation and product pages kept simple enough to get out of the way.
