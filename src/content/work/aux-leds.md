Lighting is hard to sell in a photo. It has to be seen working. The content shows each product on a real vehicle, at night, so the result is the first thing a buyer sees.

The store is organised the way customers shop: by upgrade type and by headlight size, with a search bar always in reach and the flagship series leading the homepage.
