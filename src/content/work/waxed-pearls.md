Candles are a mood product. The content leans into light and texture: soft backgrounds, a single flame, and objects that look like small sculptures. Reels show the idea in motion, with short on-screen hooks.

The storefront follows the same low light, with a dark, warm palette, a single call to action on the first screen and a WhatsApp button always in reach.
