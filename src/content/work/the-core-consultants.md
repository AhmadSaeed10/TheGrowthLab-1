Immigration content fails when it feels like paperwork. The posts lead with one clear question or claim, then give the detail in a format people can scan: a comparison table, a three-item list or a short carousel.

A consistent gradient identity and one recognisable logo badge keep every post unmistakably the same brand, even when the topic changes.
